# Image Generation Prompts

Concept image only; not fabrication authority. Do not include cut-ready dimensions, hole locations, tuning charts, or toolpaths.

## Hero Prompt

Photorealistic 3/4 studio product photograph of a finished slip-cast ceramic udu drum, medium family member: rounded Cone-6 stoneware vessel body with a top mouth opening, one smooth side hole, softly rounded hand-contact rims, subtle foot pads or stable base area, and an exterior-only glaze that leaves the playable port edges comfortable and tactile. The object should read as a Nigerian clay vessel drum, not a generic vase: closed resonant body, playable mouth and side-hole geometry, warm ceramic surface, modest handmade variations, realistic tabletop-to-lap scale. Clean studio background, soft directional studio lighting, 50mm lens, high detail, photorealistic.

## In-Context Prompt

Photorealistic ceramic studio scene of the udu being played by hand on a low stand or lap-height cushion, one hand hovering over the top mouth and the other near the side hole for slap and pitch-sweep technique. Include plaster mold halves, a sealed 3D-printed master, leather-hard clay tools, and a kiln shelf in the environment as quiet production context. Natural window light, grounded workshop storytelling, respectful presentation of the instrument's Igbo lineage, no marketing logos.

## Detail Prompt

Photorealistic macro close-up of the udu side-hole and mouth area: smooth rounded ceramic side port edge, glazed exterior surface, slightly unglazed or satin playable rim, visible wall thickness impression without measurement marks, and subtle clay speckle from fired stoneware. Shallow depth of field, soft side light, high detail, tactile ceramic texture.

## Negative / Avoid

No text, labels, watermarks, brand marks, extra limbs, impossible holes, vase-only silhouette with no side port, fantasy tribal symbols, cutaway dimension callouts, DXF-like line art, or fabrication measurements; hands must be anatomically plausible if shown.
